Frequently Asked Questions

Payment is made securely after the provider confirms task completion. We simulate UPI/card/net banking payment. Workers receive funds within 24 hours.

Workers must submit a selfie (via camera), a valid government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License, Passport or Voter ID), and verify their phone via OTP. Profiles are reviewed within 24 hours and marked Verified.

When a worker accepts a task, they receive a unique QR code with a security PIN. On arrival at the job site, they show this QR to the provider. The provider scans or enters the PIN to confirm the right person has arrived before work begins.

You can set a custom radius from 1–20 km. Only freelancers within that radius will be notified about your task. Default is 10 km. Adjust using the slider on the Nearby Tasks board.

Yes! Signing up is completely free for both job providers and freelancers. A small platform fee is charged only on successful task completion. No subscription, no hidden charges.
